### English (EN)
```
Gardina is a management system for curtain and drapery salons.
Run the entire client lifecycle from your phone: from first inquiry to the
installation of finished curtains.

Features:
• Clients & deals — one client database and a deal pipeline.
• Photo measurements — record window dimensions in mm, mounting type, and
  attach photos on site.
• Fabric & service catalog — pricing by products, variants, and services.
• Production — order statuses: cutting, sewing, quality check, ready.
• Installation — schedule and track installations.
• Analytics — team, sales, and client metrics.
• Notifications for important events.

Gardina is a B2B app for salon staff. An organization account is required.
Subscriptions are handled on the Gardina web platform.
```
